# Della's website — how to put it online (plain steps)

You only ever need the **`site` folder**. Everything the website needs is inside it.
(The `sources` folder has your original photos. Don't upload that one — the phone left hidden location data in them.)

## Put it online (about 2 minutes, free)
1. Unzip `della-antony-site.zip`. You get one folder.
2. Go to **app.netlify.com/drop** and make a free account.
3. Drag that folder onto the page. Netlify gives you a web address. That's your website.
4. Open the address on your phone and have a look.
