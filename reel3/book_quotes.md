# Water, stillness and acceptance lines from the story Della wrote

These are copied word for word from her manuscript. Swap any of them into the reel.

**Used in Reel 3**
- "We bury secrets in us and believe we all hold the same ones." (Lotus)
- "But you can't drown the oceans or hide the cloak of invisibility." (Lotus, trimmed to the first half)
- "We receive the grace of acceptance. With acceptance, comes a calm." (La Voie passage)
- "We listen with a stillness." (La Voie passage)

**Spare lines**
- "He had an ocean in his eyes that only the seekers would find." (Lotus)
- "Haven't we all waited so long for the sun and forgotten about it right before the dawn?" (Lotus)
- "I would raise an invisible wave from the bottom of my life and sent it out to the universe."
- "And we sit still as we dive into the insides of us." (La Voie passage)
- "the laws of this life dictates that the only way forward is through." (La Voie passage)
- "That the true la voie is through." (La Voie passage)
- "So let's not beat ourselves over drowning in the illusion."
- "All along we set sail to find ourselves. The greatest joy in the world has always been meeting ourselves."
- "Forgetfulness is the test. To remember the truth is the point." (Memory)
