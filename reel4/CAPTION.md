# Reel 4: Reverie, a day in the dress (caption draft)

She bought bread, rode by the sea, and went home in a painting.

Reverie is an artwork of my mother's kitchen door, left open onto the shed outside. My parents came home to India from Europe, and this is what home looks like from inside.

Here it becomes an abstract art dress: the house on the bodice, the black and yellow leaves on the skirt and back. For now it's a design sketch, wearable art India can actually wear one day. {{Della to confirm: is a Reverie dress planned?}}
