CAPTION
It’s about a boy who loved Love so much he became the Truth. Some believed he loved Love so much because he was the Truth.
“Why does Truth love Love more than anything?”
“Because there is nothing truer in the world than Love and we fall in love with those who reflect our best.”
Then Sock looked into my eyes, the way he does so nothing escapes his notice and asked, “But why does Love love Truth the most?”
I looked down at my hands quickly, “I don’t know. Because Truth is beautiful?”
Sock laughed. “That’s a statement. Why not Beauty then?”
“I really don’t know. I guess it would depend upon the kind of person Love is. ”
“Alright. Fair enough. If you were Love,” he smiled, “tell me, why would you love Truth the most?”
Sock caught me there. I got lost in that idea. To be inexplicably in love with Truth. I would have to look so deep inside me to understand why I fell in love with Truth and not anyone else. What a wonderful way it would be to figure out who I am. Sock tried to bring me back. “Think Now. Dream after.”
“Because” I paused in an attempt to hide my embarrassment. “In a world filled with darkness, no one believes in Love. She is invisible. Only Truth and those who love him sees her for who she is. Even Beauty cannot see Love without knowing Truth. Love is the truth. Without Truth, the world can’t see Love.”
“That’s right.”
“Now I can’t seem to remember why Truth loves Love.”
“The old answer would no longer suffice.
Why don’t you tell me again?” Sock looked at me with love and what seemed like a bit of pity. Did he pity me?
“Truth loves Love”,
I worded them carefully as I gathered my thoughts,
“Because she is the absolute truth of his world. He knows that darkness is the greatest illusion and he is alone in a world that which do not believe in Love because when the world don’t see Love, they don’t see the real Truth either. Love sees Truth first for who he is.” I felt a burn right in the middle of my chest.
“They are the same, aren’t they? That’s why.
When they love the other, they love themselves more.”

The rest of the chapter:
“Now what a terrible world it would be if love was taught wrong.”
My heart became quiet.
It was a horrible thought.
The world became quiet in my head.
Why would he say it out loud? “Truth would never find her.” He ended monotonously.
“No.”
We don’t end in sadness.
I knew that much.
I forgot who Sock was and that he was wiser than me.
“The world might not see her but if Truth remembers who he is, he will always find her.”
I swear in that moment Sock had glowing eyes. Did I teach the wisest man or did he fool me? I like to believe I showed him something he already knew but didn’t believe. Isn’t that what they say?
Hope is what will save the world.
